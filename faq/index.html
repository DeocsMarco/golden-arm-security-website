FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Golden Arm answers

What clients ask most often.

Answers on licensing, pricing, deployment, guard coverage, and contracts.

Are you licensed and certified?

Yes. Golden Arm is fully licensed and certified under SOSIA and DOLE. We keep our licenses and our guards' credentials current.

How many years of experience do you have?

Golden Arm is a young company, but our operations are led by people with decades of experience in the security industry.

Can you plan coverage around our specific site?

Yes. We plan the guard coverage around your site — your entrances, hours, and risks — rather than using a fixed template.

How is pricing worked out?

We serve businesses of every size. Pricing depends on your site, the number of posts, and the hours you need. Tell us about your site and we'll build a quote around it.

How fast can you deploy guards?

It depends on your location and how many guards you need. Tell us your site and timeline, and we'll give you a realistic start date.

What happens if a guard doesn't show up?

We arrange cover for planned leave. If a guard is unexpectedly absent, we coordinate a replacement from our standby pool.

Is there a minimum contract or number of guards?

No. There's no minimum contract and no minimum number of guards. We'll cover a single post or a large multi-site account.
